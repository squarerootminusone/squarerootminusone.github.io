Research

VLA scaling with newer VLM backbones

August 2026 — in progress

Swapping the vision–language backbone under a fixed action expert, to separate what a policy gains from a stronger perceptual prior from what it gains from more action data.

What vision–language–action models fail to ground

February – April 2026

A Go-stone placement benchmark showing that state-of-the-art VLA policies collapse to zero success under visual perturbation, because they never ground the coordinate named in the instruction.

Reproducing physics-informed diffusion models

May – August 2026

Replicating PDE-constrained diffusion across five variants, then halving the Darcy residual error and cutting training from 13 hours to 44 minutes.

An image processing pipeline for satellite and aerial imagery

December 2025 – January 2026

Hyperspectral preprocessing, zero-shot single-image super-resolution and spectral-angle mineral mapping over AVIRIS-NG airborne surveys.

A control dataset for non-local transition structure

June 2026

A gridworld with wormholes that tests whether a convolutional value predictor learns non-local transitions or quietly falls back on spatial proximity.

Data and computational efficiency in temporal action localization

June 2023

Which temporal action localization models hold up when training data, or training time, is the binding constraint.

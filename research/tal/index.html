Learning curves for performance- and data-efficient TAL

July 2023 · TU Delft Computer Vision Lab · advised by A. Lengyel and O. Strafforello · code

Temporal action localization models are compared on full datasets and unlimited compute, which is not the regime most people train them in. We measured data efficiency by training on progressively reduced datasets, and computational efficiency by processing videos of varying length, then derived development practices from the result.

Findings

  • TemporalMaxer outperforms the alternatives in data-limited settings
  • TriDet is the better choice when training time is the binding constraint
  • TemporalMaxer also has the lowest computational requirements at inference, which its straightforward design accounts for

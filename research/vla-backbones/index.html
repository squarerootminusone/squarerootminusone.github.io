VLA scaling with newer VLM backbones

August 2026 — in progress

A controlled backbone-swap study for vision–language–action policies: holding the action expert and the training recipe fixed while exchanging the underlying vision–language model. The aim is to separate what a policy gains from a stronger perceptual prior from what it gains from more action data — two things that current scaling results confound, because newer policies typically arrive with both at once.

Arms run on SmolVLA and PaliGemma backbones and are evaluated on LIBERO.

Notes

  • lerobot patches for architecture resolution, expert pinning, and adapter re-initialisation
  • Reproducible provisioning on rented Blackwell GPUs, with EGL/MuJoCo rendering pinned into the image — headless rendering on rented hardware is the part that reliably breaks
  • PaliGemma arms train at native batch 64 on a 96 GB card; SmolVLA-native arms fit comfortably on 32 GB

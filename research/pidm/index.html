Reproducing physics-informed diffusion models

May – August 2026 · after Bastek, Sun & Kochmann, ICLR 2025 · full write-up · code

Whether a diffusion model can carry a PDE constraint in its training objective, rather than being corrected after the fact. We replicated all five variants — plain diffusion, PG-Diffusion, CoCoGen, PIDM-ME and PIDM-SE — on Darcy flow and topology optimisation.

We added Sliced Wasserstein Distance alongside residual error and test loss, because a model can drive its residual down while its sample distribution quietly drifts; residual error alone does not catch that. The reproduction held, and the ablations found the method sensitive to residual-loss weighting and to the training noise schedule. Reduced-precision training turned out to act as a regulariser rather than a cost.

Results

  • Residuals matched the published figures to within 3%
  • Darcy residual error 1.2 × 10−2 → 5.5 × 10−3 (≈56% reduction) from hyperparameter search
  • Training wall-clock 13 h → 44 min — hardware selection, compiled kernels, bf16 precision
  • bf16 training acts as regularisation without degrading evaluation quality

Limitations

  • Ablations covered Darcy flow only; topology optimisation was left unoptimised
  • A possible pressure-field collapse needs investigating before the method is applied more broadly
